Our Story

Riverside Bakehouse has been your friendly neighbourhood bakery store available since 2015 baking fresh bread and pastries for the community

Our Mission

To bring the warm, home-style baking to our community using traditional, recipes, and quality local ingredients

Our Vision

To become the go-to bakery for authentic South African baked goods, known both locally and beyond for quality, consistency, and heart
